What does AURA actually do?

AURA takes live microphone audio, emphasizes speech frequencies, reduces some steady noise and quiet gaps, blends captured microphone audio back in, and sends it to your headphones.

Care starts gently, Focus emphasizes speech more, and Commute includes more surrounding sound. These are adjustable presets. AURA does not use cloud AI, identify or isolate a particular speaker, or transcribe speech. The bundled YAMNet model estimates vocals, vehicles, air/wind and sirens offline. It can be wrong and never separates or protects those sounds.

Device noise suppression is off by default. If you enable it and Android supports it, it can also affect the sound used for the surroundings blend. Sound quality depends on your microphone, headphones, surroundings, and phone. It cannot remove every kind of noise.

Listening comfortably and safely

Keep both app and phone volume low. Stop if sound becomes harsh, uncomfortable, or starts to echo. Move your phone away from headphone speakers if you hear feedback.

Surroundings blend mixes in microphone audio; it does not guarantee that you will hear alarms, vehicles, or people. Do not rely on AURA for safety, driving, or crossing roads.

AURA is an experimental listening aid, not a medical device or a replacement for hearing care. For hearing concerns, consult a qualified professional.

Why is there an echo or a delay?

Audio processing and your headphones introduce delay. Bluetooth usually adds more delay than wired or USB-C audio; some devices are unsuitable for comfortable live listening.

Try wired headphones, lower the volume, and move the phone closer to the person speaking. Close other apps using the microphone. If another app, a call, or a route change interrupts listening, check your headphones and start again.

The Processing figure is time spent processing a block of audio. It does not measure total microphone-to-ear delay.

Install and update on Android

Use Android 8.0 or newer. Copy the AURA APK from your build to your phone, open it with Files, and allow “Install unknown apps” for that file manager when Android asks. Install, then open AURA.

You can turn that install permission off afterward. No Play Store account, trial, or subscription is required. Only install an APK from a source you trust.

Build instructions are included in the project README. Keep the same app ID and signing key to install an update over an existing version. Export a backup first; uninstalling removes local profiles and history.

Privacy, backups, and your data

Audio is processed in memory on this phone. AURA does not save recordings, transmit audio, or require an account. Allow notifications to see the microphone service’s Stop control while listening. If notifications are denied, Android may show the service only in its active-apps controls.

Settings, profiles, and session details are stored locally. Session details include time, duration, starting settings, context, optional feedback, and notes. JSON exports contain this data, never audio. Account offers an optional Supabase cloud backup of the same metadata; nothing is uploaded automatically.

Deleting a profile does not delete past sessions. Clear history removes session details; Reset AURA removes app preferences, profiles, and history. Delete exported files in their storage location and cloud backups in Account. Reset on this phone does not delete your cloud account or sign you out.
